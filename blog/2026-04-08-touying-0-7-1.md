Touying 0.7.1 is a small follow-up release, focused on keeping content inside the slide it belongs to.

<!-- truncate -->

## What's New

- **Overflow handling**: `breakable` and `clip` options to avoid slide overflow ([#336](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/336)). With `breakable: false` a slide is constrained to a single page instead of spilling onto a new one, and `clip` decides whether the excess is truncated or merely left to overhang.
- **`lazy-layout`**: new `lazy-v` and `lazy-h` components, plus `lazy-layout`, for equalising the heights of multiple columns (or the widths of multiple rows) ([#339](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/339)).
- **More title metadata**: `config-info` gained `contact` and `extra` fields ([#342](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/342)). `extra` takes a dict that a theme reads back as `self.info.extra.key`.
- **`touying-get-config`**: a function for reading the current configuration ([#333](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/333)).

## Fixes

- `fit-to-height` and `size-to-pt` are fixed, and text is allowed to reflow ([#332](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/332)).
- Waypoint markers are fixed ([#341](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/341)).
