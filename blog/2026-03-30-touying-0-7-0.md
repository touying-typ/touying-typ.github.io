Touying 0.7.0 is a large release built around one major feature — **named waypoints** — together with a long list of animation and layout fixes.

<!-- truncate -->

## Named waypoints

Animations have always been addressed by subslide number, which means counting `#pause`es by hand and renumbering everything whenever you insert one. Named waypoints ([#298](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/298), by [@zral0kh](https://github.com/zral0kh)) let you name a position in the animation instead.

`#waypoint(<label>)` marks a spot — and, like `#pause`, advances to the next subslide unless you pass `advance: false`. A waypoint covers every subslide from its declaration until the next waypoint or the end of the slide, so passing the label to `uncover`, `only`, `effect` or `alternatives` shows content for exactly that stretch. Two markers turn a waypoint into an open range: `from-wp` is visible from it onwards, `until-wp` up to just before it. Labels need only be unique within a single slide, and they may be referenced before they are declared.

```typst
#uncover(until-wp(<summary>))[Shown before the summary.]

Main content.

#waypoint(<summary>)

Summary text. #pause More summary.

#uncover(from-wp(<summary>))[Visible from the summary on.]
```

That is three subslides: the first shows the intro line and the main content, the second swaps it for the summary, the third adds the rest — and nothing had to be numbered. `get-first` and `get-last` pick out a single edge of a waypoint, `prev-wp` and `next-wp` shift a reference by whole waypoints, and hierarchical labels such as `<part:intro>` are combined when you refer to `<part>`. The `start` parameter sets where a waypoint begins independently of where it sits in the content ([#304](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/304)), and waypoints work in `handout-subslides` too.

## Also new

- **More flexible subslide numbers**: specifications now accept `auto` and the `"h"` ("here") string, with an inverse function for string subslide numbers and waypoints ([#301](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/301)).
- **Animation functions via reducer**: functions based on `fn-wrapper` are now implicitly allowed through the reducer ([#300](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/300)), making custom animation helpers easier to build.

## Fixes

- **Covers**: `cover-with-rect` no longer breaks long lines of text when they are partially hidden, and colour and alpha covers gained fallback functions ([#328](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/328)). Cover spacing around lists was also fixed ([#303](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/303)).
- **Show rules**: ghost slides caused by show rules are gone, and show rules and the `defer` keyword are handled consistently ([#317](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/317)).
- **Headings**: `display-current-heading` uses explicit numbering when `style: auto` ([#329](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/329)).
- **Layout**: `alternatives-match` respects `stretch` ([#320](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/320)), and page margin merging and precedence are correct ([#322](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/322)).
- Plus `alert` no longer delayed ([#316](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/316)), negative subslide indices parsed correctly for `handout-subslides` ([#307](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/307)), and `slide` updating via a scoped import ([#310](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/310)).
