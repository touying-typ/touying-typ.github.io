Touying 0.6.2 is a small maintenance release, mostly about getting Touying working smoothly on Typst 0.14.

<!-- truncate -->

## What's New

- **Typst 0.14 compatibility**: fixed `magic.bibliography-as-footnote` ([#249](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/249)) and the breakage of the `theorion` package on Typst 0.14.0 ([#237](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/237)).
- **Customisable checkerboard**: `components.checkerboard` now takes `columns`, `rows`, `alignment`, `primary` and `secondary` ([#161](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/161)).
- **Full-width headers**: ratio and relative margins are now supported ([#256](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/256)).
- **`#meanwhile` in CeTZ**: fixed ([#205](https://github.com/touying-typ/touying/pull/205)).

Plus a handful of smaller fixes: the correct circle symbol, equation colour overrides via regex, `show-hide-set-list-marker-none` with a full enum, and better caching after removing the `dump` and `label-it` helpers.
